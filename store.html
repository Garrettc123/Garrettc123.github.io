Questions

What you should know.

Are all 45 digital products for sale today?

No. The separate Product Factory storefront is not verified as live. These are truthful previews with proposed pricing and inquiry links. Do not treat listed items as ready for checkout or download.

Can Garcar deploy agents and automate decisions independently?

Capabilities vary by system. Existing code includes governed execution patterns, but production access, approvals, authentication and runtime results must be independently verified. No claim is made that every agent is live.

Do I need to send credentials with my inquiry?

No. Share a non-sensitive overview of the workflow and your software. Secure data transfer and permissions are defined only after a qualified scope and agreement.

Can you work with my current CRM?

Where the vendor's APIs, permissions and configuration support it, the preferred approach is to extend the existing CRM rather than force a migration.

What is available for direct checkout?

The existing $497 review and $2,500 installation pages have payment links. Confirm fit, available delivery, written terms and any review credit before payment. No business outcome is guaranteed.
