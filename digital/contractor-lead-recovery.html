10-point lead recovery check

  1. Can you identify each inquiry's original source and received time?
  2. Does every open inquiry have a named, accountable owner?
  3. Can actual outbound contact be distinguished from notes or assumptions?
  4. Are opt-outs and do-not-contact records excluded from outreach?
  5. Can you verify permission for each proposed contact channel?
  6. Does each active record have a dated next task or documented close reason?
  7. Can you determine what is overdue from your own written policy?
  8. Are duplicates matched using source identifiers, not names alone?
  9. Can each recommended action be traced to original system evidence?
  10. Can a manager report counts without inventing potential sales or revenue?
