What the $497 review actually looks at

We look at one recent list of new leads from one place — usually Zillow or your website. We do not dig through your whole history. We do not accuse agents of skipping calls.

  • One lead source at a time
  • One recent list, usually the last 30 days, up to 500 names
  • When the lead came in, who owns it, and whether it is still open
  • How fast someone responded — only if your list shows calls, texts, or emails
  • Whether a manager should look at an old open lead — only if you tell us how many days is too long
  • If the list cannot show a call, we write “cannot tell from this list.” We do not write “nobody called.”

What you get back

1. A marked lead list

A spreadsheet of the names we reviewed. If something is flagged for a manager look, that is because it is older than the number of days your team chose. “Sat too long” never means “was never called” unless the list actually shows call times.

2. A one-page count

How many leads had an owner. How many were still open. How old they were. How much of the list could not show whether anyone called.

3. Written rules for your current system

Who owns a new lead, how long they have, who steps in if they are in a showing, and what the manager should see. You can use this even before we install anything.

A flag on the list means “look at this record.” It does not mean the assigned agent failed to call. If your list has no call times, we cannot see what happened on the phone.
